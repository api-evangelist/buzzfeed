# BuzzFeed

> BuzzFeed Inc. (Nasdaq: BZFD) is a digital media company founded in 2006, home to the flagship BuzzFeed site, HuffPost, and Tasty. It publishes viral entertainment, news, quizzes, pop-culture, and commerce content across web, video, podcasts, and social. BuzzFeed does not operate a general-purpose public developer API program; its public machine-readable surface is content syndication (RSS), an advertising/partnerships business, and a security.txt vulnerability-disclosure program via HackerOne.
